# Coaching Frameworks — Overview

Life coaches draw on several established frameworks to structure conversations, deepen insight, and support client action. This overview maps the main models used in professional coaching practice.

## GROW Model

Goal → Reality → Options → Will. A four-stage structure for goal-focused sessions. Best for problem-solving, performance planning, and career decisions. See the dedicated GROW guide for stage-by-stage questions.

## Motivational Interviewing (MI)

A collaborative style for resolving ambivalence and strengthening intrinsic motivation. Core skills: OARS (Open questions, Affirmations, Reflective listening, Summaries). Use when the client is stuck, conflicted, or knows what to do but is not doing it.

## CBT-Informed Coaching

Applies cognitive behavioural principles to identify unhelpful thinking patterns (catastrophising, all-or-nothing thinking, mind-reading) and reframe them through Socratic questioning. For coaching performance and wellbeing — not clinical treatment.

## Solution-Focused Coaching

Focuses on exceptions, future solutions, and small steps rather than analysing problems. Key questions: "When is the problem least present?" and "What would a half-point improvement look like?"

## Strengths-Based Coaching

Builds on existing client strengths, past successes, and resources rather than deficit-fixing. Pairs well with intake assessments and confidence-building work.

## SMART Goals

Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound criteria for turning aspirations into trackable commitments. Often used inside GROW at the Goal and Will stages.

## Active Listening and Powerful Questions

Cross-cutting skills rather than sequential models. Active listening creates safety; powerful open questions drive discovery. Underpin all framework-based work.

## Choosing a framework

| Client situation | Framework to consider |
|-----------------|----------------------|
| Clear goal, needs structure | GROW |
| Ambivalence, low motivation | Motivational Interviewing |
| Self-doubt, negative thinking | CBT-informed |
| Overwhelmed, many life areas | Wheel of Life + SMART |
| Repeated missed commitments | Accountability + GROW Will stage |
| Intake and balance assessment | Wheel of Life, strengths inventory |

Coaches often blend frameworks within a single session — for example, MI to explore resistance, then GROW to plan action.
