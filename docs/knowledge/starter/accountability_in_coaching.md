# Accountability in Coaching

Accountability in coaching means helping clients make clear commitments, follow through on action items, and review outcomes without judgment. The coach holds the structure; the client owns the actions.

## How accountability works

1. **Clear commitments**: Vague intentions ("I'll try to exercise more") become specific promises ("I will run twice this week, Tuesday and Thursday morning").
2. **Client ownership**: The client chooses the action and timeline — prescribed homework from the coach reduces buy-in.
3. **Obstacle planning**: Anticipate barriers. "If rain stops your run, what is your backup plan?"
4. **Check-in rhythm**: Review commitments at the start of each session. Celebrate progress; explore setbacks with curiosity, not blame.
5. **Self-accountability tools**: Journals, trackers, accountability partners, or calendar blocks the client selects.

## Coaching questions for accountability

- What will you commit to before our next session?
- How will you know you have done it?
- What might get in the way, and how will you handle that?
- On a scale of 1–10, how committed are you? What would raise that by one point?
- Who else can support you, and have you asked them?
- What did you learn from what did not get done?

## Accountability vs. pressure

Healthy accountability:
- Curious about obstacles: "What happened?" not "Why didn't you?"
- Separates the person from the behaviour.
- Adjusts goals when life context changes.

Unhealthy pressure:
- Shaming missed commitments.
- Coach-imposed deadlines the client did not agree to.
- Ignoring systemic barriers (health, workload, family crisis).

## Link to SMART goals and GROW

Accountability connects to the **Will** stage of GROW and to **Time-bound** elements of SMART goals. Progress logs and weekly commitment trackers (see homework exercises) reinforce follow-through between sessions.

## When accountability is the focus

- Client repeatedly sets goals but does not act.
- Mid-engagement when initial motivation fades.
- After breakthrough insights — convert insight into committed next steps.
- End of session action planning in every standard coaching session.
