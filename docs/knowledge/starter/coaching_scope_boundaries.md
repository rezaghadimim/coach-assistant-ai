# Coaching Scope and Boundaries

When should a coach refer a client to a therapist? Refer to a licensed mental health professional when the client's needs go beyond life coaching — for example clinical depression, trauma processing, self-harm risk, or conditions requiring diagnosis and medication. Coaches do not provide clinical treatment.

Professional life coaches work within clear boundaries. Understanding scope protects clients, maintains ethical practice, and defines when to refer to other professionals.

## What life coaching covers

- Personal and professional goals, habits, and performance
- Career transitions, leadership development, and work-life balance
- Stress management, productivity, and accountability (non-clinical)
- Relationships, communication, and life transitions in a coaching context
- Documentation and retrieval of client notes and progress (in this application)

## What is outside coaching scope

- Diagnosing or treating mental health disorders (depression, anxiety disorders, PTSD, etc.)
- Prescribing or advising on medication
- Crisis intervention for self-harm, suicide, or acute psychiatric emergency
- Legal, financial, or medical advice outside the coach's qualification
- Therapy for trauma processing — refer to a licensed therapist

## When to refer to clinical support

Refer when the client shows:
- Persistent symptoms that interfere significantly with daily functioning
- Self-harm ideation or substance dependence requiring treatment
- Trauma responses that need specialised therapeutic intervention
- Statements suggesting they need diagnosis or medication management

Suggested language: "What you are describing sounds like it may need support from a mental health professional. I can continue coaching on [specific area] alongside that, if helpful."

## Coach role vs. therapist role

The coach is a thinking partner for growth and action — not the client's therapist. Responses should be framed as guidance for the coach to use with clients: techniques, questions, and frameworks — not direct clinical treatment.

## Boundaries in this application

Coach Assistant AI supports coaches with client management, session prep, coaching strategies, and knowledge retrieval. It does not replace professional judgment, clinical training, or emergency services.
