# Strengths-Based Coaching Approach

Strengths-based coaching focuses on what clients do well, the resources they already have, and how they can build on past successes. Rather than fixing deficits, the coach helps the client identify and leverage existing strengths to reach their goals.

## Core principles

- **Every client has strengths**: Even in difficult situations, clients have used skills, relationships, or qualities that helped them cope or progress.
- **Energy follows strength**: Work aligned with natural talents tends to be more sustainable and motivating.
- **Past success predicts future capacity**: Examining when things went well reveals patterns the client can reuse.
- **Language matters**: Frame challenges as "How can we use your strengths here?" rather than "What is wrong with you?"

## Coaching techniques

### Strengths inventory
Ask the client to list moments when they felt effective, proud, or alive. Identify recurring strengths: persistence, empathy, analytical thinking, creativity, calm under pressure.

### Best-self reflection
"When you are at your best, what are you doing? How do others experience you in those moments?"

### Resource mapping
"What skills, people, experiences, or values can you draw on for this challenge?"

### Success story analysis
Pick a past achievement. Walk through what the client did, what strengths they used, and how those same strengths apply to the current goal.

## Coaching questions

- What has worked for you in similar situations before?
- When have you handled something difficult well — what did you draw on?
- What do others consistently appreciate about you?
- What energises you, and how can we build more of that into your plan?
- What strength have you been underusing lately?

## When to use strengths-based coaching

- Client focuses heavily on failures or self-criticism.
- Building confidence before a stretch assignment or career move.
- Complementing CBT-informed work — balance thought challenging with evidence of capability.
- Onboarding and intake sessions to establish a positive foundation.

## Relationship to other frameworks

Strengths-based coaching pairs well with GROW (Reality and Options stages), solution-focused coaching (exceptions and what works), and motivational interviewing (affirmations of client effort and ability).
