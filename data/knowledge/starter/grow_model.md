# GROW Model — Coaching Framework

The GROW model is a structured four-stage conversation framework for goal-focused coaching sessions. It guides the coach and client from aspiration to concrete commitment.

## Stages

### G — Goal
Establish what the client wants to achieve, both for the session and for the longer term.

Key questions:
- What do you want to achieve in today's session?
- What is the ultimate goal you are working toward?
- How will you know when you have reached it?
- What does success look like for you in three months?
- On a scale of 1–10, how important is this goal to you right now?

Tips: Make goals SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound). Distinguish between end-state goals (what the client wants) and performance goals (what they need to do to get there).

### R — Reality
Explore the current situation, facts, and context honestly and without judgment.

Key questions:
- What is happening right now with respect to this goal?
- What have you already tried?
- What is working, even partially?
- What obstacles are you facing?
- What resources — skills, relationships, time, money — do you have available?
- Who else is involved, and how are they affected?

Tips: Encourage specifics and concrete examples. Challenge assumptions gently. Avoid jumping to solutions.

### O — Options
Generate a wide range of possible actions or strategies before evaluating them.

Key questions:
- What could you do about this situation?
- What else? (repeat to expand the list)
- If you had no constraints, what would you try?
- What have you seen others do in similar situations?
- What are the pros and cons of each option?
- Which option feels most energising?

Tips: Use brainstorming without judgment. The coach can offer options with permission ("Would it be helpful if I suggested something?") but should not dominate. Evaluate options only after the list is exhausted.

### W — Will / Way Forward
Convert insight into a committed action plan.

Key questions:
- Which option will you act on?
- What is the very first step, and when will you take it?
- On a scale of 1–10, how committed are you to doing this?
- What might get in the way, and how will you handle it?
- What support do you need?
- How will you hold yourself accountable?

Tips: Low commitment (below 7) is a signal to revisit the options or reality stages. Encourage the client to set their own timeline, not the coach's.

## When to use GROW
GROW is best suited for:
- Goal-setting sessions at the start of a coaching engagement
- Problem-solving when the client has a clear challenge
- Performance improvement planning
- Career transition and decision-making

## Common pitfalls
- Rushing through Reality to get to Options — slowing down here often unlocks the real issue.
- Coach suggesting options before the client has exhausted their own — this reduces ownership.
- Skipping the Will stage — without commitment, insights rarely translate to action.
